If a website is translated with Tolgee, its owner can let the people who use it help with the translations. When you spot wording there that is wrong, awkward, or missing in your language, you can propose a better version without leaving the page you found it on.

It takes three steps, and you only do the first two once.

## 1. Install the browser plugin

Tolgee Tools lets you edit and suggest translations directly on a website.

- [Add it to Google Chrome](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/tolgee-tools/hacnbapajkkfohnonhbmegojnddagfnj)
- [Add it to Mozilla Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/tolgee-tools/)

## 2. Sign in with a Tolgee account

Open the website you want to translate, click the Tolgee Tools icon in your browser toolbar, and choose `Connect to Tolgee`. Sign in, or [create a free account](https://app.tolgee.io/sign_up) if you don't have one, and confirm the access the plugin asks for.

You don't need an invitation, and you don't need anything from the website's owner.

## 3. Suggest a better translation

The website's text is now editable.

1. Hold `Alt` (`Option` on a Mac) and move the mouse over the text you want to fix. It gets highlighted.
2. Click it. The `Quick translation` dialog opens, with the key's description under its name when there is one, so you can check what the string is for.
3. Type your version and click `Suggest`.

That's it. You can suggest as many strings on that website as you like.

## What happens to your suggestion

Your suggestion doesn't go live on the website straight away. It waits for someone who maintains the translations to accept it, and only then does it replace the text everyone sees. You can see the suggestions other people have made on the same text, and you can delete your own while it waits.

## If it doesn't work

- **The plugin doesn't offer `Connect to Tolgee`, or signing in fails.** The website isn't set up for signing in this way, or it uses its own older Tolgee server. Ask the people who run it to follow [Community Translation in Context](./projects_and_organizations/community_translation_in_context.mdx).
- **Nothing gets highlighted when you hold `Alt`.** Reload the page after installing the plugin, and check that the plugin is connected.
- **You can open the dialog, but there is no `Suggest` button.** The website's project may have suggestions turned off, or your account may not be allowed to suggest there. [Troubleshoot missing suggestions](/js-sdk/in-context#troubleshoot-missing-suggestions) goes through the causes.
