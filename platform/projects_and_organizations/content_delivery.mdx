Content Delivery will deploy your localization files to reliable and fast cloud storage so you can confidently use them in your application. This way, the updates in your localization files can be reflected without changes to your code or rebuilding the application.

:::info Feature availability
In the Free plan, you can use only one content delivery configuration,
and you cannot use custom storage.

Custom storages are available in the Team plan and higher. Multiple content delivery configurations are available in the Business plan and higher. [Upgrade your plan](https://tolgee.io/pricing) to use these features.

If you use the self-hosted version, you can connect to your own storage in [server configuration](/platform/self_hosting/configuration#tolgee-_-content-delivery).
With [Tolgee license](/platform/self_hosting/licensing) you can use Hosted Contend Delivery (same as on cloud).
:::

## Setting up the Content Delivery

You can set up Content Delivery in the `Developer settings` section of the Tolgee platform.

1. Go to Developer Settings

<ScreenshotWrapper
  src="/img/docs/platform/content-delivery/developer-settings.png"
  alt="Developer Settings"
/>

2. Click the `+ Content Delivery` button

Give the configuration a name and choose the `Languages` and `Format` of the published files. Below them, you can choose what gets published (see [Choose what gets published](#choose-what-gets-published)). These settings work the same way as on the [export page](/platform/projects_and_organizations/export).

When the `Publish automatically` option is enabled, your localization files will be published automatically after every
change in your localization data. If you deselect the checkbox, you will have to publish your localization files
manually.

<ScreenshotWrapper
  src="/img/docs/platform/content-delivery/add-content-delivery.webp"
  alt="Add content delivery dialog"
/>

3. Use the Content Delivery link

Copy the link to your Content Delivery.

<ScreenshotWrapper
  src="/img/docs/platform/content-delivery/content-delivery-link.png"
  alt="Content Delivery link"
/>

The easiest way to connect Content Delivery with Tolgee SDK is through `BackendFetch` plugin.

```tsx
const tolgee = Tolgee()
    ...
    .use(BackendFetch({ prefix: 'https://cdndev.tolg.ee/i18n/b53fb7fb98a57fd1f2ffc85ef5ee7b03' }))
    ...
```

This way, your localization files are stored outside your application, so you can update them independently from your release cycle.

:::info
Content delivery is made to be rapidly available all around the world, so we prioritize caching and using CDN over fast deployment. This means it can take up to 15 minutes (usually faster) for your changes to be published.
:::

> Read more about [providing Tolgee SDK with static data](/js-sdk/providing-static-data).

## Choose what gets published

Your app usually should not load every string in the project. Unfinished copy, strings for a feature that has not shipped yet, or strings that belong to another app can end up in front of users once the files are published. In the content delivery dialog, you can limit what gets published with the same filters you know from the [export page](/platform/projects_and_organizations/export#configure-advanced-settings). They are always visible in the dialog, below `Languages` and `Format`.

- `States` selects which [translation states](/platform/translation_process/translation_states) are published. Unlike the export page, content delivery publishes only `Translated` and `Reviewed` translations by default, so untranslated strings never reach your app.
- `Namespaces` selects which [namespaces](/platform/translation_keys/namespaces) are published. It is only shown when namespaces are enabled for the project.
- `Message & placeholder format` and format specific options such as `Support arrays` or `Escape HTML` are only shown for formats that support them.
- `Include keys with tags` publishes only keys that have at least one of the listed [tags](/platform/translation_keys/tags).
- `Exclude keys with tags` skips keys that have any of the listed tags.

Both tag fields offer the tags that already exist in the project and accept `*` as a wildcard. Leave them empty to publish all keys.

For example, imagine your production app should only get strings that are part of a release, and never strings your team still marks as `wip`. Add `release-*` to `Include keys with tags` and `wip` to `Exclude keys with tags`. Every key you tag for a new release, such as `release-2.5`, is included the next time the files are published, without editing the configuration, while unfinished keys stay out of your app until the `wip` tag is removed.

<ScreenshotWrapper
  src="/img/docs/platform/content-delivery/content-delivery-filters.webp"
  alt="Content delivery dialog with Translated and Reviewed states and tag filters release-* and wip"
/>

:::info Branching
When branching is enabled, each content delivery configuration gains a **Branch** field and is pinned to a specific branch. See [Content Delivery and Branches](/platform/branching/content_delivery) for details.
:::

## Storage file structure

Your localization files are simply named by the language tags in the root folder of your storage link with the file extension (depending on your settings):

```
├── <your Content Delivery link>
│   ├── cs.json
│   └── en.json
```

If you use namespaces, it should look like this:

```
├── <your Content Delivery link>
│   └── common
│       ├── en.json
│       └── de.json
```

## Using custom storage

:::info Feature availability

Custom delivery storages are available in the Team, Business, Advanced, and Enterprise plans. [Upgrade your plan](https://tolgee.io/pricing) to use this feature.

If you use the self-hosted version, you need to [set up the license](/platform/self_hosting/licensing) to use this feature.
:::

You can also connect the content delivery to your custom storage.

1. Open Content Storage settings

<ScreenshotWrapper
  src="/img/docs/platform/content-delivery/content-storage.png"
  alt="Content Storage"
/>

2. Add new storage

Tolgee supports S3 buckets and Azure blob storage. Fill in the settings that you obtain from your provider.

You can also test if your configuration is valid with the `Test` button.

<ScreenshotWrapper
  src="/img/docs/platform/content-delivery/add-content-storage.png"
  alt="Add Content Storage"
/>

3. Select the storage in the Content Delivery

After you create custom storage, the `Storage` field will appear in Content Delivery settings, so you can select your storage over the default one.

<ScreenshotWrapper
  src="/img/docs/platform/content-delivery/content-storage-select.png"
  alt="Content Storage select"
/>
