Translating a website with Tolgee means suggesting better wording directly on a website whose
owner lets their users help with translations. It is for anyone who spots text that is wrong,
awkward, or missing in their language. You install the Tolgee Tools browser plugin, sign in with a
Tolgee account, and Alt+click the text to propose your version. The website's maintainers review
your suggestion and decide whether it goes live.

It takes three steps, and you only do the first two once.

## Installing the Browser Plugin

Tolgee Tools is the browser plugin that lets you edit and suggest translations directly on a
website. Install it from your browser's store.

- [Add it to Google Chrome](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/tolgee-tools/hacnbapajkkfohnonhbmegojnddagfnj)
- [Add it to Mozilla Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/tolgee-tools/)

## Signing In with a Tolgee Account

Signing in tells the website's Tolgee project who you are, so your suggestions carry your name.
You don't need an invitation, and you don't need anything from the website's owner. Connect the
plugin in four steps.

1. Open the website you want to translate.
2. Click the Tolgee Tools icon in your browser toolbar and choose `Connect to Tolgee`.

   <ScreenshotWrapper
     src="/img/docs/platform/oauth/tolgee-tools-sign-in.webp"
     alt="The Tolgee Tools popup before signing in, with the Connect to Tolgee button"

3. Sign in. Most websites use Tolgee Cloud, where you can
   [create a free account](https://app.tolgee.io/sign_up). A website that runs its own Tolgee
   server sends you to that server instead, and you need an account there.
4. Click `Allow` on the `Allow access` screen. Leave the permissions as they are: if you remove the
   permission to delete your own suggestions under `Modify`, you can't take a suggestion back.

## Suggesting a Better Translation

Once the plugin is connected, the website's text becomes editable for you. Suggest a change in
three steps.

1. Hold `Alt` (`Option` on a Mac) and move the mouse over the text you want to fix. It gets
   highlighted.
2. Click it. The `Quick translation` dialog opens, with the key's description under its name when
   there is one, so you can check what the string is for.
3. Type your version and click `Suggest`.

You can suggest as many strings on that website as you like.

## What Happens to Your Suggestion

Your suggestion doesn't go live on the website straight away. It waits for someone who maintains
the translations to accept it, and only then does it replace the text everyone sees. You can see
the suggestions other people have made on the same text, and you can delete your own while it
waits, as long as you allowed the plugin to delete your suggestions when you signed in.

The website's owner reviews your suggestion in Tolgee, where everything you can do as a contributor
is described in [Community Translation](./translation_process/community-translation.mdx#for-contributors).
There you also find out how to list every project you have suggested on.

## Fixing Common Problems

Find the line that matches what you see, and do what it says.

- **The `Connect to Tolgee` button is disabled, under the note `Sign-in not available on this
  site`.** The website doesn't tell the plugin which Tolgee project it belongs to. Tell the people
  who run the website, and point them to
  [Community Translation in Context](./translation_process/community-translation-in-context.mdx).
- **Signing in fails or ends in an error.** The website's Tolgee server may be too old or not set
  up for signing in this way. Tell the people who run the website; the same page explains what
  they need to change.
- **Nothing gets highlighted when you hold `Alt`.** Reload the page after installing the plugin,
  then click the Tolgee Tools icon and check that it shows you as connected.
- **The plugin shows `Sign-in needs a newer Tolgee SDK` instead of `Connect to Tolgee`.** The
  website uses an older version of the Tolgee SDK. Tell the people who run the website that it
  needs `@tolgee/web` 7.3.0 or newer.
- **You can open the dialog, but there is no `Suggest` button.** The website may use a Tolgee SDK
  older than 7.3.0, the project may have suggestions turned off, or your account may not be
  allowed to suggest there. Nothing on your side changes any of these. Tell the people who run the
  website what you see, and point them to
  [Troubleshooting Missing Suggestions](/js-sdk/in-context#troubleshooting-missing-suggestions).
