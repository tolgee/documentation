A community profile is a public page in Tolgee that shows the work one user has done in community
projects. It lets contributors show their translation work. It also tells project owners who is
behind a suggestion. Tolgee builds the profile from the user's
suggestions, translations, reviews and comments. The profile becomes public with the first
contribution, and its owner can hide it at any time.

A community project is a project that its owner opened to
[Community Translation](./community-translation.mdx). Work in other projects never appears on a
community profile. When an owner
[closes a project to the community](./community-translation.mdx#making-a-project-private-again),
the work done in it disappears from the profile too.

## What Does a Community Profile Show?

A community profile shows who the contributor is and what they have done in community projects.
Everything on it comes from community projects only, including the work the person did there as a
team member. The profile has these parts.

- The name and avatar of the user, and the month of their first contribution
- `Projects`, the community projects the user has contributed to, each linked to its community
  project page
- Four totals: `Suggestions`, `Accepted`, `Acceptance rate` and `Contributions`. A contribution
  is a translation, a [suggestion](/platform/projects_and_organizations/suggestions), a review or
  a [comment](./comments.mdx) made by hand.
- A calendar of one year that shows how active the user was each day. You can switch it between
  `Suggestions`, `Accepted` and `Contributions`, and between the current and the previous year.
- `Recent activity`, the latest things the user did, each with the key, the text and the project

The acceptance rate compares accepted suggestions with all suggestions, including the ones still
waiting for review. A contributor whose suggestions have not been reviewed yet has a low rate
through no fault of their own.

## Where Do I Find a Community Profile?

A community profile lives at `/users/` followed by the ID of the user, for example
`https://app.tolgee.io/users/1234`. On a self-hosted instance, the profile is at the same path on
your own address. Anyone can open a public profile without an account. Tolgee links to profiles
from these places.

- The contributor cards on the [contributors page](./community-translation.mdx#seeing-who-has-contributed) of a community project
- The [card that opens](./community-translation.mdx#reviewing-community-suggestions) when you
  hover over the avatar of a suggestion's author in a community project, through its
  `View profile` link
- The names in the `Community` tab of the project's
  [Members](/platform/projects_and_organizations/members#community-contributors) page
- Your own account settings, through `Open profile`

The cards link to a profile only when it is public. The names in the `Community` tab always
link, so a hidden profile opens with the message `This profile is not available`.

To share your own profile while it is public, open it and click the copy icon next to your name,
with the tooltip `Copy profile link`.

## Who Can See a Community Profile?

A community profile is either public or private, and its owner decides. Until you contribute to
a community project, you have no public profile. After your first contribution, the profile is
public unless you hide it. If every community project you contributed to is later closed, you
are back to having no public profile. A profile is in one of two states.

- **Public.** Anyone can open the profile, with or without a Tolgee account.
- **Private.** Only you can open the profile. Everyone else sees `This profile is not available`.
  You see your own profile with the note `Your profile is private. Only you can see it.`

Hiding the profile hides only the profile page. Your name still appears next to your suggestions
and comments, on the contributors page of the project, and in the project's activity.

## Hiding or Showing Your Community Profile

Hide your community profile when you do not want other people to see a summary of your work
across projects. You can show it again at any time. You need to be logged in, and you need at
least one contribution to a community project. Until then, the switch is disabled with the note
`Your profile appears after your first contribution.`

To change who can see your profile, follow these steps.

1. Click your avatar in the top right corner and choose `Account settings`. The `User profile`
   page opens.
2. Turn the `Public community profile` switch off to hide the profile, or on to show it. The line
   under the switch says who can see the profile: `Anyone can see your profile.` or
   `Only you can see your profile.`
3. Click `Save`.
