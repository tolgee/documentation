A community profile is a public page in Tolgee that shows the work one user has done in community
projects. It lets contributors show their translation work. It also tells project owners who is
behind a suggestion. Tolgee builds the profile from the user's suggestions, translations, reviews
and comments. The profile becomes public with the first contribution. Its owner can hide it at
any time.

:::info
On a self-hosted instance, community profiles need Tolgee v3.244.0 or newer.
:::

## What Does a Community Profile Show?

A community profile sums up what a person has done in
[community projects](./community-translation.mdx): the projects they helped, how many of their
[suggestions](/platform/projects_and_organizations/suggestions) were accepted, and their recent
activity. Work done there as a team member counts too. When an owner closes a project, its work
leaves the profile.

## Where Do I Find a Community Profile?

To open your own profile, click `Open profile` next to the `Public community profile` switch in
your account settings. Tolgee links to the profile of a contributor from the places that show
their name in a community project.

## Who Can See a Community Profile?

Anyone can open a public profile, with or without a Tolgee account.

Hiding the profile hides only the profile page. Your name still appears next to your suggestions
and comments and on the contributors page of the project.

## Hiding or Showing Your Community Profile

Hide your community profile when you do not want other people to see a summary of your work
across projects.

1. Click your avatar in the top right corner and choose `Account settings`.
2. Turn the `Public community profile` switch off to hide the profile, or on to show it.
3. Click `Save`.

## FAQ

### How Do I Make My Profile Private?

Go to `Account settings`, turn off the `Public community profile` switch, and click `Save`.
Your profile page is then hidden, but your name still appears next to your suggestions and
comments. See [Hiding or Showing Your Community Profile](#hiding-or-showing-your-community-profile).
