A community profile is a public page in Tolgee that shows the work one user has done in community
projects. It lets contributors show their translation work. It also tells project owners who is
behind a suggestion. Tolgee builds the profile from the user's suggestions, translations, reviews
and comments. The profile becomes public with the first contribution, and its owner can hide it
at any time.

## What Does a Community Profile Show?

A community profile sums up what a person has done in
[community projects](./community-translation.mdx): the projects they helped, how many of their
[suggestions](/platform/projects_and_organizations/suggestions) were accepted, and their recent
activity. Only work in projects that are open to the community counts, including work done there
as a team member. When an owner closes a project, its work leaves the profile.

## Where Do I Find a Community Profile?

A community profile lives at `/users/` followed by the ID of the user, for example
`https://app.tolgee.io/users/1234`. On a self-hosted instance, it is at the same path on your own
address. Tolgee links to profiles from the
[contributors page](./community-translation.mdx#seeing-who-has-contributed) of a community
project, from the [author of a suggestion](./community-translation.mdx#reviewing-community-suggestions),
and from the `Community` tab of the
[Members](/platform/projects_and_organizations/members#community-contributors) page.

## Who Can See a Community Profile?

A community profile is either public or private, and its owner decides. Until you contribute to
a community project, you have no public profile. After your first contribution, anyone can open
your profile, with or without a Tolgee account, unless you hide it. A hidden profile opens only
for you.

Hiding the profile hides only the profile page. Your name still appears next to your suggestions
and comments and on the contributors page of the project.

## Hiding or Showing Your Community Profile

Hide your community profile when you do not want other people to see a summary of your work
across projects. You can show it again at any time. The switch works once you have contributed
to a community project.

1. Click your avatar in the top right corner and choose `Account settings`.
2. Turn the `Public community profile` switch off to hide the profile, or on to show it.
3. Click `Save`.
